# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a rigorous, welcoming community where people can question assumptions and
improve the design together. Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and in good faith;
- critique ideas and evidence rather than people;
- welcome questions from different experience levels;
- disclose important limitations and conflicts of interest;
- protect private data and security reports;
- give credit for ideas, experiments, code, and documentation;
- help turn disagreement into testable alternatives.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate humiliation,
doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
community calls, and other spaces representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit
or remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, or ban a participant when
necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially and
fairly as practical.

Maintainers are also subject to this standard. A maintainer with a conflict should
recuse themselves from the response.
