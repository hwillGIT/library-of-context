# Code of Conduct

## Community standard

The project supports a rigorous and welcoming community. Participants can question
assumptions and improve the design together. Participants must:

- communicate respectfully and in good faith.
- critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- welcome questions from different experience levels.
- disclose important limitations and conflicts of interest.
- protect private data and security reports.
- give credit for ideas, experiments, code, and documentation.
- help turn disagreements into testable alternatives.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate humiliation,
doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and
community calls. It also applies in other spaces that represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit
or remove content. They may issue a warning, limit participation, or ban a participant
when necessary. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially and fairly as
practical.

This standard also applies to maintainers. A maintainer with a conflict should not take
part in the response.
